## How to Use This Reference

These three curated walks are written for alumni visitors who want both orientation and memory. Distances and durations come from the provided route concepts. Use this file as route copy source text for the Local Guide Map.

## Walk 1, Yale Campus Greatest Hits

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Route Name | Yale Campus Greatest Hits |
| Distance | 2.5 mi |
| Duration | 2 to 3 hrs |
| Best Time | Morning to late afternoon |
| Yale Connection Note | This route covers the core spaces where first impressions of Yale become lifelong visual memory |

### Stop-by-Stop Directions

| Stop Order | Stop | Direction Cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Union Station | Start at the station and head toward the downtown center |
| 2 | New Haven Green | Use the Green as the civic midpoint before entering Yale core blocks |
| 3 | Phelps Gate / Old Campus | Continue toward College Street and enter the historic first-year quad zone |
| 4 | Sterling Library | Walk deeper into central campus toward the High Street landmark |
| 5 | Beinecke | Move north within the Yale core to the rare book library plaza |
| 6 | Cross Campus | Continue through the open campus lawn corridor |
| 7 | Harkness Tower | Follow internal campus paths to the tower axis |
| 8 | Yale Visitor Center | Finish at Elm Street for orientation materials or tours |

### Tips

- If a guided tour is planned, align this route to Yale Visitor Center timing.
- Harkness carillon concerts are useful anchor points for pacing.
- This route works well for first-time alumni family visits.

## Walk 2, Off-Campus New Haven

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Route Name | Off-Campus New Haven |
| Distance | 3.5 mi |
| Duration | 3 to 4 hrs |
| Best Time | Late morning through sunset |
| Yale Connection Note | This route shows how Yale life extends into neighborhood food streets, parks, and local rhythm |

### Stop-by-Stop Directions

| Stop Order | Stop | Direction Cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | New Haven Green | Start from the central Green |
| 2 | Wooster Square Park | Head toward Wooster Square and transition into the Italian-American neighborhood |
| 3 | Wooster Street Pizza (Pepe's or Sally's) | Continue along Wooster Street for a core food stop |
| 4 | East Rock Park summit | Move north toward East Rock for panoramic city views |
| 5 | Pardee Rose Garden | Descend within the park area and visit the rose garden |
| 6 | Whitney Avenue | Finish along Whitney Avenue as a Yale-to-neighborhood connector |

### Tips

- This route benefits from one planned meal window on Wooster Street.
- East Rock segments are easier with comfortable walking shoes.
- Spring timing pairs especially well with nearby blossoms and park color.

## Walk 3, New Haven Pizza Trail

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Route Name | New Haven Pizza Trail |
| Distance | 2 mi |
| Duration | 3 to 4 hrs (including eating time) |
| Best Time | Lunch to early evening |
| Yale Connection Note | This route captures the food arguments, loyalties, and reunion rituals that alumni carry for years |

### Stop-by-Stop Directions

| Stop Order | Stop | Direction Cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Frank Pepe's | Start on Wooster Street at the historic anchor |
| 2 | Sally's Apizza | Continue along the same corridor for direct comparison |
| 3 | Modern Apizza | Move toward State Street for the third major style benchmark |
| 4 | BAR | Return toward Crown Street for mashup pizza and social energy |
| 5 | Wooster Square Park | End in the park to reset and reflect after tastings |

### Tips

- Portion strategy matters, share pies to compare more styles.
- Peak times can stretch waits, off-peak windows are smoother.
- This walk is ideal for small groups that want conversation-heavy pacing.
