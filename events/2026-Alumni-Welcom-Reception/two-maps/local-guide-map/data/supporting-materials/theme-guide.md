## Theme Core

"Two Cities, One Community" ties together memory and momentum. The Local Guide Map invites alumni to hold two places in view at once, New Haven and Shanghai, then see one shared Yale story across both.

## City Meaning

- New Haven is the city that made us Yale. It carries the places where friendships formed, late-night meals became tradition, and daily routines turned into lifelong memory.
- Shanghai is the city that brings us together. It is the present-day chapter, where alumni reconnect, build new friendships, and continue Yale community life in a global context.

## How the Map Works

The Local Guide Map includes a city toggle. Users switch between New Haven and Shanghai while keeping the same navigation logic, so they can compare and connect experiences across both cities.

## Shared Category System

Both cities use the same six categories:

| Category | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| food | Signature meals, neighborhood staples, alumni gathering spots |
| culture | Museums, history, landmarks, institutions |
| art | Galleries, performance spaces, design-forward venues |
| nature | Parks, waterfronts, scenic walks, seasonal spaces |
| practical | Orientation stops, transit, visitor logistics |
| nightlife | Evening social spots and after-hours energy |

## Visual Direction

The map follows a Magritte surrealist visual identity. The direction uses a cool palette, twilight slate foundations, sky blue accents, and hard-edge reveals to create a dreamlike but precise city-reading experience.
