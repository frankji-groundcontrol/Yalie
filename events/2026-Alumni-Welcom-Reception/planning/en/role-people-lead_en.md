## 1. Role Mission & Success Outcomes
Mission: The People Lead owns the full volunteer pipeline and front-of-house guest experience, making sure the right people are in the right roles and every attendee feels welcomed from the moment they arrive.
- 12-15 confirmed volunteers, including backups, are locked by T-1 week.
- All volunteers pass readiness gates by T-3 days, aligned with `plan_en.md` Section 5.
- Check-in runs smoothly with no queue longer than 3 minutes.
- Every attendee is greeted and directed within 60 seconds of arrival.

## 2. Scope, Authority, and Non-Goals
- I decide: volunteer roster changes, backup activations, check-in procedures, walk-in guest policy, and badge logic.
- I recommend: staffing model changes to the Event Lead and role-fit suggestions to other leads.
- I must escalate: volunteer no-shows beyond backup capacity to the Event Lead, and any VIP or sensitive guest issue to the Event Lead.
- Not my job: program timing (Program Lead), tech support (Ops/Tech Lead), and final event calls (Event Lead).

## 3. Lifecycle Ownership Matrix
| Event Phase | My Mode | What I Own | Handoff To/From |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup | Support | Confirm volunteer arrival, distribute role assignments and badges | From all leads |
| Arrival | Primary | Run check-in desk, manage guest flow, VIP flagging, walk-ins | To Event Lead for escalations |
| Opening + Sharing | Support | Off-duty from check-in, monitor floor for late arrivals | Hand check-in to floater |
| Fishbowl | Off-duty | Escalation-only support, no active check-in ownership | From floater if issue appears |
| Transition | Support | Help direct attendees into group clusters when needed | To Program Lead + Ops/Tech Lead |
| Co-Creation | Off-duty | Escalation-only support, floaters may assist tech pods | From Ops/Tech Lead for people issues |
| Wrap-up + Teardown | Support | Assist closing flow, collect volunteer feedback | To Event Lead for debrief |

## 5. Team Pods & Direct Reports
- Direct volunteer pods: Check-in & Welcome (2 people), Floaters/Backups (2 people).
- Pre-event needs: each pod member reads their role brief, understands check-in flow, and knows escalation path.
- During event: check-in pod owns the arrival window, then one rotates to floor support after peak.
- Floaters are cross-trained for any pod, including temporary support to logistics or tech flow if asked.
- Backup trigger: if a check-in volunteer is absent, activate the first available floater immediately, then notify Event Lead if the floater pool is exhausted.

## 6. Escalation Rules
- Others escalate to me when a volunteer is absent or late, check-in system issues appear, walk-in guest decisions are needed, badge or guest list discrepancies surface, or any front-of-house confusion appears.
- I escalate to Event Lead when 2 or more volunteers are no-shows and backups are used up, when there is a VIP or sensitive guest situation, or when any issue cannot be resolved within 3 minutes.

## 7. If Things Go Wrong, 3 Scenarios
1. Check-in bottleneck at arrival. Trigger: queue exceeds 3 minutes. First 5 minutes: open second check-in lane with floater, simplify to pre-printed badge process. Notify Event Lead. Fallback: switch to name-list-only check-in and skip badges.
2. Multiple volunteer no-shows. Trigger: 3 or more confirmed volunteers absent by T-60 minutes. First 5 minutes: activate all backups, merge check-in and logistics pods if needed. Notify Event Lead. Fallback: Event Lead authorizes role merging across leads.
3. Uninvited or disruptive guest. Trigger: someone not on the guest list insists on entry, or a guest disrupts flow. First 5 minutes: explain policy calmly and offer organizer contact. Notify Event Lead immediately. Fallback: Event Lead makes the final call.

## 8. Day-of Quick Card
- T-180 min: Arrive, confirm volunteer attendance, distribute assignments.
- T-120 min: Test check-in flow with Ops/Tech Lead, confirm guest list is loaded.
- T-30 min: Brief check-in pod, confirm floater positions.
- Arrival window: Run check-in desk, flag VIPs and issues.
- Post-arrival: Hand check-in to one floater, shift to floor observation.
- Fishbowl/Co-creation: Stay available for escalation, assist transition grouping when needed.
- Wrap-up: Collect volunteer feedback, confirm all volunteers are accounted for.
- Post-event: Send volunteer thank-you and share debrief notes with Event Lead.
