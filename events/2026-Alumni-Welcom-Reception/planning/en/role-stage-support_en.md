## 1. Role Mission & Success Outcomes
Mission: Keep the stage running smoothly behind the scenes, handle mic handoffs, timing cues, and speaker transitions so attendees see a polished program.
Headcount: 2 volunteers. Reports to: Program Lead (流程负责人).
Success outcomes: every mic handoff is seamless, timing cues are visible to speakers, no awkward gaps appear between segments.

## 2. Scope & Boundaries
- I do: manage mic flow between speakers, show timing cue cards, support clicker/slides, guide speakers to position, support Fishbowl facilitator with audience mic.
- I don't do: decide program changes, managed by Program Lead; manage guests, managed by People Lead; troubleshoot tech, managed by Ops/Tech Lead.
- I escalate to Program Lead when: a speaker is missing or late to stage, AV equipment fails, a segment runs significantly over time, or Fishbowl participation needs intervention.

## 3. Lifecycle Ownership Matrix
| Event Phase | My Mode | What I Do |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Support | Test mic, clicker, timing display, and speaker seating with Program Lead. |
| Arrival | Off-duty | Review run-of-show one final time and stay ready. |
| Opening + Sharing | Primary | One volunteer handles mic handoffs, one handles timing cues, both signal speakers on time. |
| Fishbowl | Primary | One handles audience mic for open participation, one tracks time and cues moderator. |
| Transition | Support | Help direct attendees into groups if Program Lead requests support. |
| Co-Creation | Off-duty | Stay available as a floater if a blocker appears. |
| Wrap-up | Primary | Support demo presentations, manage final mic flow, assist closing sequence. |

## 4. Personal Timeline & Deliverables
| When | What I Need To Do | Done When |
|---|---|---|
| T-7 days | Confirm role in writing | Confirmation posted in group chat |
| T-5 days | Read run-of-show, speaker order, time blocks, cue signals | I can describe the full flow from memory |
| T-5 to T-3 days | Attend briefing and rehearsal with Program Lead | Mic handoff sequence and timing cues practiced |
| Day-of T-120 min | Arrive and test AV with Program Lead | Mic, clicker, and timing display all working |
| Day-of T-30 min | Final speaker check with Program Lead and partner | Speaker positions and cue signals confirmed |

## 5. Who I Work With
- Partner: the other stage support volunteer, one owns mic flow and one owns timing, split is confirmed with Program Lead.
- Lead: Program Lead owns run-of-show and all timing decisions, I follow their cues exactly.
- Fishbowl facilitator/moderator: I support audience mic flow during open discussion.
- Backup: if one stage volunteer is absent, the remaining volunteer focuses on mic flow first, Program Lead handles timing cues directly.

## 6. Escalation Rules
- I handle myself: routine mic handoffs, normal timing cues, and guiding speakers to position.
- I escalate to Program Lead: speaker missing or late, AV failure, segment running 5+ minutes over, Fishbowl participation stalling.
- I escalate to Event Lead through Program Lead: major AV failure affecting the whole program.

## 7. If Things Go Wrong, 2 Scenarios
1) Mic dies mid-segment: immediately pass a backup mic to the speaker.
If no backup is available, speaker projects voice while Ops/Tech Lead is notified, keep body language calm so the audience sees a smooth swap.
2) Speaker runs 5+ minutes over: show a clear wrap-up timing card.
If ignored after 1 minute, signal Program Lead for direct intervention, I do not interrupt the speaker myself.

## 8. Day-of Quick Card
- T-120 min: test AV and practice mic handoff sequence.
- T-30 min: confirm final speaker positions and review cue signals.
- Opening: manage mic flow and timing cues through sharing block.
- Fishbowl: one handles audience mic, one tracks time for moderator.
- Transition and wrap-up: assist grouping if asked, then support demo mic flow and closing.
