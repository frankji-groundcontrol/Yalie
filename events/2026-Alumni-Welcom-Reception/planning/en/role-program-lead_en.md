## 1. Role Mission & Success Outcomes
Mission: The Program Lead owns the event's narrative arc on stage, from opening through Fishbowl to the critical transition into co-creation, ensuring every phase starts on time, flows naturally, and protects the energy that carries attendees from discussion to action.
Success outcomes are measured against run-of-show execution in `plan_en.md`.
- Every program segment starts within 5 minutes of scheduled time.
- Fishbowl to OpenClaw transition completes with no more than 5 minutes of dead air.
- Speakers are cued smoothly, with no awkward gaps between segments.
- Wrap-up includes demo showcase and proper closing.

## 2. Scope, Authority, and Non-Goals
- I decide: program timing adjustments, speaker order changes, Fishbowl duration cuts, transition script execution, shortened-variant activation.
- I recommend: fallback mode switch to Event Lead if program timing is severely affected.
- I must escalate: program delays exceeding 10 minutes to Event Lead; any speaker issue that affects narrative arc to Event Lead.
- Not my job: volunteer roster, owned by People Lead.
- Not my job: tech troubleshooting and venue setup, owned by Ops/Tech Lead.
- Not my job: final event decisions, owned by Event Lead.

## 3. Lifecycle Ownership Matrix
| Event Phase | My Mode | What I Own | Handoff To/From |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup | Support | Confirm speaker arrival; test mic, clicker, and timing cues with stage volunteers | To Program Support pod |
| Arrival | Off-duty | Available for escalation; review final run-of-show | From People Lead |
| Opening + Sharing | Primary | Cue speakers, manage mic flow, keep time, signal transitions | With Stage & Program Support |
| Fishbowl | Primary | Manage open participation, enforce time gate, prep group formation with facilitator | With facilitator/moderator |
| Transition | Primary | Execute transition script with Ops/Tech Lead; signal fixed guides to receive groups | To Ops/Tech Lead |
| Co-Creation | Off-duty | Available for escalation; monitor timing for wrap-up start | From Ops/Tech Lead |
| Wrap-up | Primary | Cue demo presentations, manage closing flow, deliver or introduce closing remarks | To Event Lead for close |

## 4. Personal Timeline & Deliverables
| When | Required Output | Done When | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-4 weeks | Draft run-of-show with time blocks | Shared with Event Lead for review | Speaker confirmations |
| T-3 weeks | Confirm speaker order and Fishbowl moderator | All speakers confirmed in writing | Speakers |
| T-2 weeks | Finalize cue sheet with speaker names, time cues, transition scripts | Printed and shared with stage pod | Run-of-show approval |
| T-2 weeks | Write Fishbowl to OpenClaw transition script and shortened variant | Reviewed with Ops/Tech Lead | Ops/Tech tech readiness |
| T-1 week | Rehearse with stage support volunteers and facilitator | All cues tested end to end | Stage volunteers confirmed |
| T-5 to T-3 days | Brief stage support pod on run-of-show, mic flow, escalation | Pod confirms understanding | People Lead roster |

| Post-event 24h | Share timing notes and lessons learned | Notes sent to Event Lead | Personal notes |

## 5. Team Pods & Direct Reports
- Direct volunteer pod: Stage & Program Support, 2 people.
- Coordination partner: Fishbowl facilitator/moderator, not a volunteer role, may be a speaker or invited guest.
- Pre-event baseline: stage pod knows mic handoff sequence, timing cue signals, and how to handle a speaker running over.
- During event split: one volunteer runs mic flow, one manages timing cues and clicker, Program Lead focuses on cueing and transitions.
- Backup rule: if one stage volunteer is absent, pull one floater and brief only mic flow first, then timing cues if capacity allows.

## 6. Escalation Rules
- Others escalate to me when: speaker is late to stage, mic or AV fails during a segment, Fishbowl participation stalls, or any issue affects program timing.
- I escalate to Event Lead when: program is 10 or more minutes late, a speaker cancels, Fishbowl cannot transition on time, or any issue requires a public announcement.
- I notify all leads when: program timing changes affect their phase, for example co-creation starts late.

## 7. If Things Go Wrong, 3 Scenarios
1) Speaker runs long. Trigger: sharing segment exceeds allocated time by 5 or more minutes. First 5 minutes: show timing cue card, if ignored stage volunteer signals wrap-up. Notify Event Lead. Fallback: cut next speaker's time or merge into Fishbowl opening.
2) Fishbowl runs late and threatens co-creation start. Trigger: Fishbowl reaches T-10 warning and discussion is still active. First 5 minutes: activate shortened variant, cut final audience round, use shortened closing script. Notify Ops/Tech Lead to prepare early. Fallback: co-creation start loses no more than 10 minutes, aligned with `plan_en.md` Section 8.
3) AV or mic failure mid-program. Trigger: mic cuts out during sharing or Fishbowl. First 5 minutes: stage volunteer swaps to backup mic, if no backup speaker projects voice while Ops/Tech Lead fixes. Notify Ops/Tech Lead. Fallback: if not fixed within 5 minutes, continue without mic for short periods since room size allows it.

## 8. Day-of Quick Card
- T-120 min: Test all AV with stage pod and confirm speaker arrivals.
- T-30 min: Final run-of-show review with Event Lead, brief stage pod one last time.
- Opening: Cue first speaker, manage mic flow, enforce time blocks.
- Sharing: Keep speakers on time, signal transitions between speakers.
- Fishbowl: Manage open participation, watch clock, at T-10 min signal Ops/Tech Lead.
- Transition: Execute transition script, direct attendees into groups, confirm fixed guides received groups.
- Co-Creation: Off-duty from stage, monitor timing for wrap-up start.
- Wrap-up: Cue demo presentations, manage closing flow, thank speakers.
