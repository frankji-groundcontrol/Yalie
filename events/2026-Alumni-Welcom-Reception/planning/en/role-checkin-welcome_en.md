## 1. Role Mission & Success Outcomes
Mission: I am the first face guests see, I make every attendee feel welcomed and clearly directed within 60 seconds of arrival.
Headcount: 2 volunteers in this role.
Reports to: People Lead.
Success outcomes:
- No queue longer than 3 minutes during arrival peak.
- Every guest receives a badge and knows where to go next.
- VIPs and walk-ins are handled smoothly with fast escalation when needed.

## 2. Scope & Boundaries
- I do: greet guests, check names, hand out badges, direct people to seating or refreshments, flag VIPs to People Lead, handle walk-in questions.
- I don't do: make guest list decisions, set program timing, provide tech support.
- I escalate to People Lead when: a name is not on the list, a walk-in requests entry, a guest is confused or upset, or queue wait goes beyond 3 minutes.

## 3. Lifecycle Ownership Matrix
| Event Phase | My Mode | What I Do |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Support | Help People Lead test badge flow and set up the registration desk |
| Arrival | Primary | Run check-in, greet each guest, hand out badges, direct to seating |
| Opening + Sharing | Off-duty | One of us stays near the door for late arrivals, the other rests |
| Fishbowl | Off-duty | Stay available for rapid support if People Lead requests help |
| Transition | Support | Help direct guests into group clusters when asked |
| Co-Creation | Off-duty | Assist floor logistics only if asked by People Lead |
| Wrap-up + Teardown | Support | Help collect registration desk materials and tidy the area |

## 4. Personal Timeline & Deliverables
| When | What I Need To Do | Done When |
|---|---|---|
| T-7 days | Confirm role in writing to People Lead | Confirmation appears in group chat |
| T-5 days | Read role brief and run-of-show | I can explain check-in flow and badge logic |
| T-5 days | Know escalation path | I can name People Lead and Event Lead clearly |
| T-5 to T-3 days | Attend briefing | I have face-to-face alignment with People Lead and partner |
| Day-of T-120 min | Arrive and test badge flow with People Lead | Front desk is ready for first guests |
| Day-of T-30 min | Take final brief from People Lead and take position | Registration desk is staffed and live-ready |

## 5. Who I Work With
- Partner: the other check-in volunteer, we run the desk together during arrival.
- Lead: People Lead, they own guest list setup, badge logic, and walk-in policy.
- Floaters: if arrival volume spikes, People Lead may assign a floater, I brief them on badge handoff.
- Handoff: after arrival peak, one of us stays for stragglers, one shifts to floor support.

## 6. Escalation Rules
- I handle myself: normal check-in, simple directions, friendly greetings.
- I escalate to People Lead: name not on list, walk-in request, badge or list mismatch, queue over 3 minutes, confused or upset guest.
- I escalate to Event Lead through People Lead: VIP issue, security concern, or any issue not resolved in 3 minutes.

## 7. If Things Go Wrong, 2 Scenarios
1) Queue backs up. Trigger: expected wait exceeds 3 minutes.
Action: simplify flow, switch to pre-printed badges only, ask People Lead for a floater to open a second lane.
2) Uninvited guest insists on entry. Trigger: name not on list and person is persistent.
Action: stay calm and friendly, explain I need organizer confirmation, bring in People Lead immediately, do not argue or refuse alone.

## 8. Day-of Quick Card
- T-120 min: Arrive, test badge flow, set up registration desk.
- T-30 min: Take final brief and stand by in position.
- Arrival window: Run check-in, greet, badge, direct, flag issues to People Lead.
- Post-arrival: One stays for stragglers, one shifts to floor support.
- Wrap-up: Help collect desk materials and report issues to People Lead.
