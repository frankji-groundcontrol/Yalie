## 1. Role Mission & Success Outcomes
Mission: I am the final decision-maker and external-facing coordinator, and I ensure this event delivers on its promises to attendees, partners, and the Yale alumni brand.
- Go/no-go decisions are made on time at each milestone.
- Stakeholder communication is completed before each milestone.
- Any escalation unresolved at lead level is closed within 3 minutes.
- The event delivers warm community, smooth execution, and visible outputs.

## 2. Scope, Authority, and Non-Goals
- I decide: event go/no-go, fallback mode activation, final escalation calls, and external stakeholder messaging.
- I recommend: staffing changes, People Lead decides; program timing adjustments, Program Lead decides.
- I must escalate: none in normal operation, this is the top escalation point.
- If truly stuck: consult co-organizing partners for a viability call.
- Not my job: direct volunteer management, speaker cueing, or venue setup details.

## 3. Lifecycle Ownership Matrix
| Event Phase | My Mode | What I Own | Handoff To/From |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup | Support | Monitor readiness reports from all leads | Receive go/no-go readiness from Ops/Tech Lead |
| Arrival | Support | Stay visible and available for decisions | Receive guest-flow status from People Lead |
| Opening + Sharing | Support | Observe timing risk and decision needs | Receive cue updates from Program Lead |
| Fishbowl | Support | Watch time gate with Program Lead | Prepare transition authorization |
| Transition | Primary | Authorize Fishbowl to OpenClaw go signal | Joint handoff with Program Lead |
| Co-Creation | Support | Monitor tech health and decision thresholds | Receive fallback triggers from Ops/Tech Lead |
| Wrap-up + Teardown | Primary | Authorize closing flow and event close | Ensure outputs are collected from all leads |

## 4. Personal Timeline & Deliverables
| When | Required Output | Done When | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| This week | Appoint all 4 leads | All 4 confirmed in writing | None |
| T-4 weeks | Lock date and venue | Confirmed with venue partner | Ops/Tech Lead venue check |
| T-3 weeks | Review draft roster | Gaps identified and escalated | People Lead draft roster |

## 5. Team Pods & Direct Reports
- Direct reports: People Lead, Program Lead, Ops/Tech Lead, 3 people total.
- Pre-event: I ensure each lead confirms pod volunteers and deliverables by deadline.
- During event: I remain reachable by all leads at all times.
- I do not take a volunteer function pod during live execution.
- Backup: if I am unavailable, Program Lead assumes final decision authority.

## 6. Escalation Rules
- Others escalate to me when: an issue is unresolved after 3 minutes at lead level, a mode switch is required, an external stakeholder issue appears, or any safety concern appears.
- I escalate to co-organizing partners, MuShanghai or Yale alumni association leadership, only if event viability is at risk.

## 7. If Things Go Wrong - 3 Scenarios
- Scenario 1, key speaker cancels day-of. Trigger: no-show confirmed. First 5 min: Program Lead adjusts run-of-show, I communicate to audience if needed. Notify: Program Lead. Fallback: shorten sharing block and extend Fishbowl.
- Scenario 2, venue infrastructure failure, power or AC. Trigger: venue issue confirmed. First 5 min: Ops/Tech Lead assesses scope, I decide continue, pause, or relocate. Notify: all leads. Fallback: if unrecoverable in 15 min, switch to outdoor or lobby contingency.
- Scenario 3, volunteer no-shows exceed backup capacity. Trigger: 3 or more confirmed volunteers absent. First 5 min: People Lead activates backups, I authorize role merging. Notify: People Lead. Fallback: merge check-in and logistics pods, pull one tech floater if needed.

## 8. Day-of Quick Card
- T-180 min: Arrive and confirm venue access with Ops/Tech Lead.
- T-30 min: Run walk-through with all leads and issue go-live clearance.
- Arrival window: Stay visible near entrance and greet VIPs.
- Opening: Observe from side and monitor timing risk.
- Fishbowl end: Authorize transition go signal.
- Co-creation: Monitor tech status and be ready for a mode-switch decision.
- Wrap-up: Deliver closing remarks or authorize closer, then confirm outputs are collected.
- Post-event: Thank volunteers and collect debrief notes.
