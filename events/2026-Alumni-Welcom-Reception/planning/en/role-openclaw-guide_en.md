## 1. Role Mission & Success Outcomes
Mission: I am the hands-on bridge between OpenClaw and my group cluster, helping teams start fast, stay productive, and produce something visible by the end of co-creation.
Headcount model: 3 fixed guides total, each covering a cluster of 2-3 groups.
Reporting line: I report to the Ops/Tech Lead for zone assignment, support mode, and escalation.
Success means: groups launch within 5 minutes of transition, every group ships a demo or prototype, and non-tech participants stay included and contributing.

## 2. Scope & Boundaries
- I do: help groups install and use OpenClaw, suggest project directions, keep energy up, track outputs, and include non-tech participants.
- I don't do: fix infrastructure issues (rover handles), make program decisions (Program Lead), or manage guest flow (People Lead).
- I escalate to rover when: a group is stuck on install or setup for more than 5 minutes, Wi-Fi is unstable, or I hit a tool bug I can't resolve.
- I escalate to Ops/Tech Lead when: rover cannot clear the blocker, or multiple groups are stuck at the same time.

## 3. Lifecycle Ownership Matrix
| Event Phase | My Mode | What I Do |
|---|---|---|
| Setup through Sharing | Off-duty | Ensure my own OpenClaw setup works and review my assigned group zone. |
| Fishbowl T-10 min | Prep | Move visibly to my group zone when signaled by Ops/Tech Lead. |
| Transition | Primary | Receive my group cluster immediately, settle teams, introduce OpenClaw, and get first actions started. |
| Co-Creation | Primary | Guide groups through the session, troubleshoot basic issues, keep momentum, and track outputs. |
| Wrap-up | Support | Help groups prepare demo or output for showcase, then hand outputs to Program Lead for demo order. |
| Teardown | Off-duty | Assist only if asked by Ops/Tech Lead. |

## 4. Personal Timeline & Deliverables
| When | What I Need To Do | Done When |
|---|---|---|
| T-7 days | Install and test OpenClaw personally. | I can demo basic features without help. |
| T-5 days | Hard gate: if OpenClaw is not installed and tested, I move to a non-tech role. | Readiness status is confirmed by Ops/Tech Lead. |
| T-5 to T-3 days | Attend rehearsal with Ops/Tech Lead. | I have practiced install path, demo path, and fallback path. |
| T-5 days | Confirm my group zone assignment. | I can point to my zone on the venue map. |
| Day-of T-60 min | Arrive and verify Wi-Fi plus OpenClaw from my zone. | I am readiness-confirmed for live support. |
| Fishbowl T-10 min | Move to my zone when signaled and stay visible. | My cluster can find me immediately at transition. |

## 5. Who I Work With
- Fellow guides: 2 other fixed guides in adjacent zones, coordinate when groups merge or a zone overloads.
- Rover: roaming troubleshooter, I call rover for install failures or bugs not solved in 5 minutes.
- Ops/Tech Lead: assigns zones, sets support mode, and makes fallback decisions.
- Backup model: if one fixed guide is absent, rover covers that zone and a floater takes roaming duty, coordinated by Ops/Tech Lead.

## 6. Escalation Rules
- I handle myself: basic OpenClaw questions, group encouragement, project direction prompts, and non-tech inclusion.
- I escalate to rover: install failure, Wi-Fi issues in my zone, or a bug I cannot troubleshoot within 5 minutes.
- I escalate to Ops/Tech Lead: rover cannot resolve, or multiple groups in my cluster are blocked at once.

## 7. If Things Go Wrong, 2 Scenarios
1) Group cannot install OpenClaw. Action: run basic checks first, restart app and device, recheck Wi-Fi, verify account path. If unresolved after 5 minutes, call rover. While waiting, shift the group to a shared-screen demo so they stay engaged.
2) Non-tech participants feel left out. Action: assign explicit roles such as idea generation, design feedback, and presentation prep. Frame it clearly, every team needs people who shape the story, not only the code.

## 8. Day-of Quick Card
- T-60 min: Confirm OpenClaw works from my zone and check Wi-Fi.
- Fishbowl T-10 min: Move to my zone and stay visible.
- Transition: Receive groups, intro OpenClaw, and launch first tasks.
- Co-creation: Guide teams, troubleshoot basics, keep momentum, include everyone.
- Wrap-up: Help groups prep demo or output, hand to Program Lead.
