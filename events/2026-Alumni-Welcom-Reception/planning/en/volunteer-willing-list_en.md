## How to Use This Document

1. Read the role summary table to understand what each role does.
2. Check the capability matrix to see which roles match your skills and availability.
3. Fill in the interest form at the bottom with your preferences.
4. Send it to the People Lead or contact `franklonely` (WeChat) / `frank.ji@aya.yale.edu`.

## Event Overview

- **What**: Yale Shanghai Alumni Welcome Reception — intergenerational sharing, Fishbowl AI discussion, and hands-on OpenClaw co-creation.
- **When**: Late April 2026 (one weekend day, exact date TBD).
- **Where**: Lujiazui Digital Intelligence Port, Shanghai (tentative).
- **Scale**: 150-200 attendees.
- **Volunteer team**: 12-15 people, all Yale alumni.

## Role Summary & Fit Guide

### Lead Roles (4 positions)

| Role | What You Do | Good Fit If You... | Time Commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Event Lead | Final decisions, escalation, stakeholder coordination | Are experienced in event management; comfortable making calls under pressure | Pre-event: ~4-6 h/week for 4 weeks. Day-of: full day |
| People Lead | Recruit volunteers, manage roster, run check-in, handle guest flow | Are organized, communicative, enjoy coordinating people | Pre-event: ~4-6 h/week for 5 weeks. Day-of: full day |
| Program Lead | Manage stage timing, cue speakers, run Fishbowl, execute transitions | Are calm under pressure, have stage or event hosting experience | Pre-event: ~3-4 h/week for 3 weeks. Day-of: full day |
| Ops/Tech Lead | Set up venue, manage logistics, ensure OpenClaw works, handle tech fallback | Are hands-on, familiar with OpenClaw or AI tools, comfortable with logistics | Pre-event: ~4-6 h/week for 4 weeks. Day-of: full day (arrive earliest) |

### Volunteer Roles (8-11 positions)

| Role | What You Do | Good Fit If You... | Key Requirement | Day-of Hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Check-in & Welcome (2) | Greet guests, hand out badges, direct people | Are warm, organized, enjoy making people comfortable | Attend briefing | Arrive T-120 min; busiest at arrival |
| Stage Support (2) | Manage mics, show timing cues, support speakers | Are calm, reliable, happy to work behind the scenes | Attend briefing + rehearsal | Arrive T-120 min; busiest during program |
| OpenClaw Guide (3) | Help teams use OpenClaw during co-creation | Have installed/used OpenClaw; enjoy teaching | **Must install and test OpenClaw by T-5 days** | Arrive T-60 min; busiest during co-creation |
| OpenClaw Rover (1) | Move between groups to fix tech issues | Are patient, strong troubleshooter, comfortable with AI tools | **Must install and test OpenClaw by T-5 days** | Arrive T-60 min; busiest during co-creation |
| Floor & Logistics (2-3) | Set up venue, manage signage/seating/power, run teardown | Are dependable, attentive to physical details | Attend briefing | **Arrive T-180 min (earliest)**; stay for teardown |
| Floater / Backup (2) | Fill any gap — step into whatever role needs help | Are flexible, quick to adapt, team-oriented | Read ALL role briefs | Arrive T-120 min; variable |

## What Happens After You Sign Up

1. **People Lead reviews** your form and matches you to a role based on your preferences, skills, and team needs.
2. **You receive a confirmation** with your assigned role and a link to your role plan document.
3. **You complete readiness gates** (briefing, rehearsal if applicable, OpenClaw install if tech role) — all before T-3 days.
4. **Day-of**: arrive at your assigned time, follow your role plan, and help make this event great.

Questions? Contact `franklonely` (WeChat) or `frank.ji@aya.yale.edu`.
