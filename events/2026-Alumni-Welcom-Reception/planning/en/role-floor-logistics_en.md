## 1. Role Mission & Success Outcomes
Mission: Keep the physical environment event-ready from setup to teardown.
You make the space work, signage stays clear, seating flows, power is accessible, and the venue looks professional throughout the day.
Headcount: 2-3 volunteers. Reports to: Ops/Tech Lead.
Success outcomes:
- Venue is fully set up by T-120 min.
- No physical issue, seating, power, or signage disrupts any event phase.
- Teardown is clean, complete, and handed back with issue notes.

## 2. Scope & Boundaries
I do:
- Set up signage, seating, power access, and physical flow.
- Manage materials and quick floor fixes during the event.
- Run teardown and restore the venue.
I don't do:
- Check-in guests, owned by People Lead and check-in team.
- Manage AV or stage flow, owned by stage support.
- Troubleshoot OpenClaw, owned by tech team.
I escalate to Ops/Tech Lead when:
- Venue equipment is missing or broken, power fails, seating cannot fit plan, or any physical safety concern appears.

## 3. Lifecycle Ownership Matrix
| Event Phase | My Mode | What I Do |
|---|---|---|
| Setup (T-180 to T-120 min) | Primary | Set signage, registration desk, seating zones, stage area, discussion zone, OpenClaw help zone, and power points. |
| Arrival through Fishbowl | Support | Monitor floor for fallen signage, blocked paths, and power needs, then fix quickly. |
| Transition | Support | Rearrange seating for group clusters when needed. |
| Co-Creation | Support | Keep power available, replace supplies, and maintain sofa discussion area flow. |
| Wrap-up | Support | Assist output collection and manage closing-flow seating. |
| Teardown | Primary | Collect signage, materials, and equipment, restore venue, and log lost items and issues. |

## 4. Personal Timeline & Deliverables
| When | What I Need To Do | Done When |
|---|---|---|
| T-7 days | Confirm role in writing | Confirmation is posted in group chat. |
| T-5 days | Read setup and teardown checklists from Ops/Tech Lead | Full sequence is understood. |
| T-5 to T-3 days | Attend briefing | Venue map is reviewed and zone assignment is clear. |
| Day-of T-180 min | Arrive first with Ops/Tech Lead and begin setup | Setup is complete and handed off by T-120 min. |
| Day-of during event | Monitor floor and resolve physical issues | Issues are fixed quickly with minimal disruption. |
| Post-event | Complete teardown and hand venue back clean | Issue log is submitted to Ops/Tech Lead. |

## 5. Who I Work With
- Fellow logistics volunteers, 1-2 peers in the same role, we split setup zones across the venue.
- Lead: Ops/Tech Lead, provides venue map, setup checklist, and teardown checklist, I follow that plan.
- Backups/floaters join logistics during teardown to speed cleanup.

## 6. Escalation Rules
- I handle myself: furniture repositioning, signage replacement, cable management, and routine floor fixes.
- I escalate to Ops/Tech Lead: missing or broken equipment, power failure, safety concern, or any issue requiring venue staff contact.

## 7. If Things Go Wrong, 2 Scenarios
- Scenario 1, Venue access delayed: cannot enter by T-150 min.
- Action: notify Ops/Tech Lead immediately and prep outside work, signage and materials sorting. If still blocked at T-120 min, Ops/Tech Lead escalates to Event Lead.
- Scenario 2, Power outlet fails during Co-Creation.
- Action: redirect groups to backup power points from power map, use extension cords, and if widespread notify Ops/Tech Lead for venue staff contact.

## 8. Day-of Quick Card
- T-180 min: Arrive with Ops/Tech Lead and begin full setup.
- T-120 min: Setup complete, hand off venue-ready status.
- During event: Monitor floor, fix issues, stay available.
- Teardown: Collect everything, restore venue, log issues.
- Post-close: Confirm clean handback to Ops/Tech Lead.
