## 1. Role Mission & Success Outcomes
Mission: You are the safety net, cross-trained to step into any volunteer function when someone is absent, overloaded, or when a phase needs extra hands.
Headcount: 2 floaters. Reports to: People Lead for assignment, then follows the lead of whichever team you join.
Success outcomes: no-show gaps are filled within 5 minutes, you step in without re-briefing, and transitions get extra support when needed.

## 2. Scope & Boundaries
- I do: fill any vacant volunteer position, support peak moments (arrival, transition, teardown), and assist whichever lead requests help.
- I don't do: make decisions for any function, or stay permanently assigned to one pod unless told otherwise.
- I escalate to People Lead when: I am unsure where needed, receive conflicting requests, or get asked to do work I was not briefed on.

## 3. Lifecycle Ownership Matrix
| Event Phase | My Mode | What I Do |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Support | Assist logistics if extra hands are needed, otherwise stand by. |
| Arrival | Support | Assist check-in if queue builds, otherwise observe and stay ready. |
| Opening + Sharing | Off-duty | Stand by near the floor and observe for issues. |
| Fishbowl | Off-duty | One floater supports Program Lead if requested, helps audience flow. |
| Transition | Support | Help direct attendees into groups and fill any transition gap. |
| Co-Creation | Support | Assist tech if a fixed guide is absent, otherwise help floor logistics. |
| Wrap-up | Support | Assist output collection and support closing flow. |
| Teardown | Primary | Join logistics team for full teardown. |

## 4. Personal Timeline & Deliverables
| When | What I Need To Do | Done When |
|---|---|---|
| T-7 days | Confirm role in writing. | Confirmation is posted in group chat. |
| T-5 days | Read all role briefs: check-in, stage, logistics, tech basics. | I understand every function at a basic level. |
| T-5 days | Learn escalation paths for all teams. | I can name every lead and their scope. |
| T-5 to T-3 days | Attend briefing. | I am cross-trained on check-in flow, mic basics, and setup checklist. |
| Day-of T-120 min | Arrive and check in with People Lead for initial assignment. | Initial assignment is confirmed. |
| Day-of ongoing | Check in with People Lead between phases for reassignment. | I am never idle longer than one phase checkpoint. |

## 5. Who I Work With
- Primary lead: People Lead, assigns me based on real-time needs.
- All other leads: when I join a team, I follow that team's lead for the assignment window.
- Fellow floater: coordinate with the other backup volunteer so we are not both idle or both in the same pod.

## 6. Escalation Rules
- I handle myself: basic tasks within the function I am filling, such as check-in and floor support.
- I escalate to the lead of the team I currently support: any function-specific issue.
- I escalate to People Lead: unsure where to go, conflicting requests, or no assignment for more than 10 minutes.

## 7. If Things Go Wrong, 2 Scenarios
- Scenario 1, multiple no-shows. Trigger: 2 or more volunteers absent. Action: People Lead assigns me to the most critical gap first, usually check-in during arrival or tech during co-creation. I stay in that role for the full phase if needed.
- Scenario 2, I am asked to fill a tech role but have not used OpenClaw. Action: tell People Lead honestly, then take non-tech tasks in that pod, such as group encouragement and zone logistics, while tech volunteers handle tool issues.

## 8. Day-of Quick Card
- T-120 min: Arrive and check in with People Lead for assignment.
- Arrival: Support check-in if needed, otherwise stand by.
- Between phases: Check with People Lead, where do you need me next?
- Transition: Help direct attendees into groups.
- Co-creation: Fill any gap in tech zone, floor, or logistics.
- Teardown: Join logistics team for full cleanup.
