## Getting Started / 如何开始

1. Read the volunteer interest form to understand what each role involves. / 阅读志愿者意向登记表，了解各岗位职责。
   - English: [Volunteer Interest Form](en/volunteer-willing-list_en.md)
   - 中文：[志愿者意向登记表](cn/volunteer-willing-list_cn.md)
2. Fill in your interest — name, availability, skills, and preferred roles. / 填写意向 — 姓名、时间、技能、偏好岗位。
3. Send to People Lead / 发给人员负责人：`franklonely`（WeChat / 微信）/ `frank.ji@aya.yale.edu`
4. Once assigned, read your role plan — it tells you exactly what to do. / 分配后阅读你的岗位计划 — 上面写了你需要做什么。
