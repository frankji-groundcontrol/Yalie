# Volunteer Call for the Yale Shanghai Alumni Gathering

We are beginning to recruit volunteers for our Yale Shanghai alumni gathering in late April, and we would love to do this together as a community.

This event is meant to be a warm, thoughtful, and lively gathering for alumni, incoming students, and invited friends. We are hoping to create a space with conversation, intergenerational exchange, and a hands-on AI co-creation session. It is also a rare mix of artists, engineers, investors, and entrepreneurs in one room, moving from a live AI fishbowl discussion into OpenClaw-enabled co-creation. To make the day feel welcoming, smooth, and genuinely collaborative, we are inviting a small group of volunteers from the Yale community to help support it.

If you would like to be part of it, here are the main volunteer roles we are currently looking for:

## 1. Check-in / Welcome

Help greet guests, support sign-in, answer basic questions, and make sure people feel comfortable as they arrive.

This role is a good fit if you are warm, organized, and enjoy helping people feel at ease.

## 2. Program / Stage Support

Help the event flow smoothly by supporting speaker transitions, timing, microphones, and other small on-site coordination needs.

This role is a good fit if you are calm, reliable, and happy to help the event move smoothly behind the scenes.

## 3. OpenClaw Guide

Support participant groups during the AI co-creation session. We especially welcome volunteers who have already installed or used OpenClaw and can help others get started on site.

This role is a good fit if you enjoy helping others learn by doing and feel comfortable guiding small groups.

## 4. Floating Tech Troubleshooter

Move across different groups during the co-creation block to help solve setup issues, unblock teams, and support the overall OpenClaw experience.

This role is a good fit if you are patient, hands-on, and comfortable solving problems in a live setting.

## 5. Logistics / Floor Support

Help with signage, seating flow, room setup, materials, and other practical details throughout the event.

This role is a good fit if you are attentive, dependable, and enjoy quietly making things run well.

## 6. General Floater / Backup

Step in wherever help is needed across welcome, logistics, and transitions.

This role is a good fit if you are flexible, responsive, and happy to support wherever needed.

## What We Ask From Volunteers

- A willingness to help create a welcoming and collaborative atmosphere
- Reliability in communication and follow-through
- Availability for pre-event briefing and day-of coordination
- For tech-related roles, comfort with OpenClaw or willingness to prepare in advance

If you are interested, please reach out and let us know:

1. which role(s) you may be interested in
2. whether you have used or installed OpenClaw before
3. whether you are available for an event in late April
